# Verify aesthetics and real interaction

Beauty, elegant interaction, and accurate relationships are joint delivery conditions. Evaluate against the user's chosen aesthetic intent and host design rather than silently substituting your preferences. For unresolved directions, explain where each candidate works. Engineering checks support these results but do not replace them. Declare relevant viewport × state × input combinations. A complete new scene usually includes desktop/narrow, initial/selected/expanded/reset, and failure/fallback states; local changes need only affected combinations.

## Inspection sequence

1. Record revision, exact dependencies, browser, viewport, DPR, and hardware/simulation conditions. Run the engineering checks the project actually provides.
2. Open the actual page, inspect console/page/network errors, and confirm the intended 3D content. A fallback surviving module failure is not a WebGL pass.
3. **Inspect real screenshots** of the full page and key action results. Judge silhouette appeal, proportion, compositional hierarchy, material/light finish, occlusion, clipping, spatial meaning, and labels. An image's existence does not mean it was reviewed.
4. **Operate the scene** with mouse, keyboard, and relevant touch paths. Are actions discoverable, progress controllable, feedback continuous, and return easy? Check drag misselection, cancellation, rapid repeats, reset, and narrow-screen access.
5. **For implementation tasks, fix the most consequential aesthetic and experience defects first**, then inspect images and actions again. Do not expand a visibly flawed representative scene or stop at functional success. For review-only tasks, record defects, impact, and suggestions without editing.
6. **Measure cost and fallback behavior** as relevant: renderer.info, frame intervals during operation, idle rendering, low quality, WebGL creation failure/context loss, asset failure, reduced motion, and exit/re-entry. State browser and simulation conditions; rAF is not a GPU profiler.

Observe animation changes over time or on video, not just endpoint screenshots. Set performance budgets for the actual devices and page; example counts are not universal thresholds.

## Non-game visual and experience criteria

Evaluate relevant items with concrete evidence and gaps, using Pass / Needs work / Unverified / Not applicable. Self-review finds defects; do not default to average scores or passing grades, or mark unknowns as observed.

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Intent and coherence | Do form, composition, materials, and actions support the intended feeling and preserve the chosen language? |
| Form and proportion | Are physical silhouettes, proportions, and negative spaces distinctive and appealing? Does abstract form encoding clarify content or merely carry interchangeable labels? |
| Composition and hierarchy | Is the initial focus clear with useful whitespace, and do selected/expanded/narrow states remain balanced? |
| Spatial accuracy | Are axes, containment, connections, order, scale, and motion correct, with schematic exaggeration disclosed? |
| Materials and lighting | Are surface hierarchy, curvature, seams, normals, and contact resolved? Do transparency and exposure support the chosen expression? |
| Operation and feedback | Are discovery, action, feedback, and return natural, consistent, and reversible? Can keyboard/touch complete core tasks? |
| Page integration | Do model and controls belong to the page, without occlusion, overflow, conflicting styles, or scroll capture? |
| Candidate differences, when choosing | Do differences answer the actual decision with comparable facts and completion? A different reading mode is not automatically a different aesthetic language. |

Consider restraint: are detail, material changes, and animation worth keeping, or would removal improve clarity and coherence? Meaningful richness is valid too. A simple work can pass every relevant criterion; there are no minimum quotas for environmental density, characters, particles, or wear. Strengths elsewhere cannot cancel obvious aesthetic defects, spatial errors, inaccessible actions, or unseen critical states. Color entropy, edge density, and polygon counts do not establish beauty.

## Report results

Lead with aesthetic/experience readiness and specific gaps, then provide revision/file hashes, executed commands, actual input, screenshots and observations, performance conditions, fallback results, and unverified areas. Distinguish automated results, judgments from actual viewing/operation, and the user's direction approval. None establishes the others.

Label static-only reviews. Screenshots do not prove interaction; simulated touch is not hardware testing; a running example does not prove automatic skill selection after installation. Plans are not passed checks, and subjective aesthetic judgments are not certification.
